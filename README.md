# Telvoro - Presence & SIP Phone browser extension — privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-09 · applies to extension version 2.5.0 and later_

The Telvoro extension is a front end for a business phone system. It handles
your data as follows.

**What it stores on your device**
- Your preferences (notification choices, pinned tabs, availability-notification
  rules) in the browser's extension storage.
- In *Team presence* mode only: the address of your phone system's XSI server
  and your own phone-system user ID and password, in the browser's local
  extension storage on this device. They are used solely to sign in to that
  server and are never sent anywhere else. Removing the extension or turning
  the mode off deletes them.

**Where data goes**
- With the Telvoro desktop app: the extension talks only to that app on your
  own computer (localhost / native messaging), including identifying itself to
  the app by its extension ID so the app can accept the connection. The app
  contacts your phone system as configured by you.
- In Team presence mode: the extension connects directly to the XSI server you
  entered, to read your company directory and receive presence events.
- The extension contacts no other server, loads no remote code, and includes
  no analytics or advertising.

**Phone numbers you click**
A number you choose to call (selected text or a tel: link) is passed to the
Telvoro desktop app on your computer to place the call. It is not stored by the
extension.

**Data we collect**
None. The developer has no server and receives no data from the extension.
